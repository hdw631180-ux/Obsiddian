Status: Proposed Policy

1. Purpose

This Security Policy establishes the security principles, requirements, and controls governing the design, development, deployment, and operation of an AI-powered Operating System (AI OS).

The primary objective is to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, availability, privacy, and reliability of the operating system, its AI components, its users, and its connected services.

Security must be integrated into every layer of the OS architecture and throughout the AI lifecycle.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

Operating system kernel and core components.
AI models and inference engines.
AI agents and autonomous workflows.
User applications and system services.
Local and cloud-based AI infrastructure.
APIs, plugins
